It sounds less strange when I point of the fact that humans keep track of time, unlike most animals.

Our ability to keep track of time, is closely related to observation of the sky, marking shadows while they move across the ground,
and attempt to divide the motion of shadows during daylight into proportional segments,
using those segments to count larger spans of time and to plan harvests and religious rituals based on this knowledge.

Without cognitive faculty to keep track of time, the invention of agriculture would be impossible.

As civilizations evolved, affiliation with the 3 schools of thought became a model for economic and political influence.

### Overview of the plan

1. First pause on AGI development: Limit all AGI research to theorem proving in Inside Theory.
2. Gradually expand AGI research to Outside Theory, but always keep weaker AGI agents than the core research of theorem proving in Inside Theory.
3. Second pause on AGI development: When Outside Theory is mastered, we limit AGI research to Path Semantics to leverage its lack of Ontology.
4. Educate people on use of Gini solvers in the economy.

Maybe our species can follow these 4 steps, which might happen in parallel, but where a lower point has always higher priority than the later points.

### Make economic progress through use of Gini solvers

In the economy, the goal is to solve constraint problems.
However, unlike domains where constraint solving is most studied, there is little room for backtracking in the economy.

Therefore, we need a good solver for constraint problems without backtracking.

A Gini solver is a stochastic binary search algorithm, that optimizes the inequality gradient, to improve the performance of the economy on constraint solving tasks.

AGI agents will probably not be good at solving problems where Gini solvers are good at, and vice versa.

At the moment, more powerful AGI agents is the least problem we have.
The most pressing problem, is lack of education about using Gini solvers in the economy.
